# Build 364 Beta Blocker Investigation

## Decision

**IMPLEMENT ONE BOUNDED BUILD 364 STRIKE.** The Founder’s physical Build 363 retest exposed three
beta-readiness blockers. Production remains frozen; this task does not authorize push, merge,
deployment, provider access, credential access, or a release-rule change.

## Founder evidence

- `IMG_7689.PNG`: shells collect, but a visible Lost Wisdom page does not respond during the
  setup-forming portion of the first-session run.
- `IMG_7690.PNG`: the once-ever FIRST WIN is a generic platform trophy plus unrelated headline and
  sentence floaters; hierarchy, authored art, contrast, and reward meaning are below the product bar.
- `IMG_7691.PNG`: the first real trade presents a visually flat path and no audible score in the
  supplied Founder preview.

## Root causes

1. `tradeInProgress()` treated any `setupFlow` as an active trade. Boxes and pages therefore stayed
   interaction-locked throughout setup formation even though no ticket or position owned the screen.
   Their collision also sampled only the current frame’s center distance, unlike the swept-body shell
   pickup, so fast shell-rolls and visible edge overlap could miss.
2. FIRST WIN was assembled from a system trophy emoji, a second trophy glyph, loose large text, and
   separate sentence floaters. It had no single art/layout owner.
3. The Founder preview command used `?fresh=1&mute=1`; the game correctly persisted `cq_music=off`, so
   the supposedly representative first-trade preview was forced silent. The first guided path itself
   only authored a shallow dip, recovery, and target run, so it did not deliver the intended emotional
   scare/profit/give-back arc.

## Accepted scope

- Interaction ownership and swept/body collision for boxes and Lost Wisdom pages.
- One code-native FIRST WIN milestone card and revised copy hierarchy.
- One dedicated first-trade music route and deterministic four-act guided candle path.
- Safe preview-link correction, proportional focused tests, Browser cases, build identity, and
  lifecycle documentation.

## Hard exclusions

No trade economics, risk sizing, stop/target values, later-trade path, curriculum, boss engine, save
schema, Finn model, art/binary asset, provider configuration, credential, production, or release-lock
change.

## Acceptance criteria

- With setup formation active but no ticket/position, one swept Finn pass breaks a box and collects a
  visible Lost Wisdom page exactly once; an actual ticket/position still defers both.
- FIRST WIN is one readable premium card at 390x844, uses a bespoke vector trophy and canonical shell,
  has no system trophy emoji or loose duplicate headline, and reports the earned shell reward.
- The first real trade selects its own audible score, reaches a near-stop scare without touching stop,
  surges materially into profit, gives back to break-even or below, and then reaches target. Ordinary
  trades retain their existing route.
- Fresh Founder preview links do not force or persist mute.
- Source/root/site artifacts are byte-identical and all focused, release-control, parity, regression,
  and rendered Browser gates pass on one exact fingerprint.
