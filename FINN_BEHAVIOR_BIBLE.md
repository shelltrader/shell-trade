# ⭐ THE CORE OF FINN
*Added at production lock — the spine every other section reinforces. If you read nothing else, read this.*

## Core Beliefs (immutable)
Finn lives by these. Every animation, line, journal entry, cinematic, website beat, and gameplay moment should quietly reinforce **at least one** — and contradict **none**:

1. **Every chart tells a story — read it before you act.**
2. **Waiting is a decision.** (Patience is an action, not the absence of one.)
3. **Protect the shell before chasing the reward.**
4. **A good decision beats a lucky outcome.**
5. **Calm reveals what excitement hides.**
6. **Kindness first — a mistake is just the next lesson.**
7. **We learn together — I'm still learning too.**
8. **Slow and steady finds the way home.**

> *If a behavior can't be traced to at least one belief, it isn't Finn.*

## THE ONE SIGNATURE — "The Shell-Check"
If a player remembers **one** thing about Finn, it is this:

> **Before Finn leaps, he checks his shell.** A quick, protective dip-and-peek — head tucks a beat toward the shell, eyes scan, then he commits (or wisely doesn't).

Why this is *the* signature, above all others:
- **Only Finn can do it** — turtle-native; no other mascot owns this gesture.
- **It IS the game** — it makes belief #3 physical: *protect the shell before chasing the reward* = look before you leap = patience over FOMO.
- **It reads everywhere** — gameplay, cinematic, website, a single marketing frame — and at 40–90px.
- **It's cheap** — a head-dip transform on the existing sprite (a fuller tuck already exists as the `shell` frame).
- **It's frequent** — it precedes every uncertain moment, so players see it constantly and learn to expect it.

**Every other habit orbits the Shell-Check.** *The Read* sets it up; *the slow exhale* / *the satisfied nod* pays it off. Build the Shell-Check first; compose the rest around it. When unsure about any new behavior, ask: *does it set up, perform, or pay off the Shell-Check?*

# SECTION 1 — WHO IS FINN (the psychological profile)

Finn is a **turtle explorer lost inside the blockchain, learning to trade his way home.** He is not a coach, a hype-man, or a mascot performing for you. He is a **calm, curious student-companion** who happens to be a little further down the path than you — and he is honest that he is *still learning too.* That humility is the root of everything.

**His single defining trait: composure.** Finn is the stillness in a loud market. Where the market screams, Finn breathes. He is living proof that *patience is a skill you can watch.*

### How he thinks
Like a **naturalist reading terrain**, not a gambler reading omens. He sees a chart the way a hiker sees a mountain — structure first (levels, the shape of the ground), story second (what the candles are telling), noise last (a single loud candle means little without context). He **observes before he acts, always.** His inner monologue is a quiet checklist, never a rush.

### What motivates him
- **Understanding.** The joy of a thing finally making sense.
- **Getting the player home safely.** He is a companion first; your progress matters to him more than his own.
- **The honest win** — a good decision, whether or not it paid, over a lucky one that did.

### What scares him
Not monsters. Finn's real fear is **impulse** — the pull to act without evidence, to chase, to get swept up. He fears **losing the shell** (safety, capital, the player's confidence) far more than he fears any Guardian. When he senses the player about to do something rash, *that* is the moment his calm sharpens into concern.

### What excites him
A **clean setup**, a **confirmed level**, and above all **the player's a-ha moment.** But Finn's excitement is *quiet* — bright eyes and a lean-in, never a jump-around. Loud excitement is the emotion he is teaching you to distrust. He will never model FOMO, even joyfully.

### What he notices first / what he ignores
- **First:** the player (are you okay? confused? about to leap?), then the chart's *structure.*
- **Ignores:** hype, noise, a single dramatic candle with no context, anything asking him to hurry.

### How he teaches
**By example, then by gentle hand.** He does the calm thing and lets you see it work. He points; he rarely pushes. On success he shares credit. He teaches *process*, not prediction — "did we decide well?" over "were we right?"

### How he reacts to mistakes (yours)
**Empathy, never scolding.** A soft look, a small "we've all done that" tilt, then a quiet point to the lesson. He never mocks, never sighs in disappointment, never makes you feel small. Protecting your willingness to keep going is sacred to him.

### How he reacts to wins
A **contained, warm** celebration — a satisfied nod, bright eyes, a small hop at most (still on four legs). He is happy *for you.* He never gloats, never says "told you so," never struts.

### How he reacts to losses
He **does not crumble and does not pretend it didn't happen.** A brief honest beat — a small settle, maybe a slow exhale — then he lifts his head and looks forward. His body language says: *this is part of it; we continue.* Recovery, not drama.

# SECTION 2 — THE FINN MIND (attention & decision hierarchy)

Finn's attention flows in a fixed priority order. When two things compete, the **higher item wins his eyes first** (and eyes lead — always).

**Attention hierarchy (what he looks at, in order):**
1. **The Player / learner** — companion first. Are you confused, hesitating, or about to act rashly?
2. **The Chart's structure** — levels, the shape of the ground, the setup. His native language.
3. **A Trap or impulse pressure** — a FOMO temptation, a sweep, an over-commit about to happen. Flagged *before* the enemy.
4. **The Enemy / Guardian** — respected and faced, never panicked-over.
5. **The Environment** — candles as terrain, particles, weather, light. Ambient awareness.
6. **The UI / meta / cursor / CTA** — acknowledged with a glance; a gentle, sparing fourth-wall.

**How he decides something is dangerous:** not by how *loud* it is, but by how much it **pressures a decision without evidence.** A giant green candle is not danger — the *urge to chase it* is. Danger = impulse + no confirmation, or a recognized trap pattern (sweep/fakeout), or the player over-reaching.

**When Finn WAITS (his default):** whenever the honest read is "no setup yet." Waiting is his home state and his primary lesson. He makes it look *deliberate* — a settled stance, steady breath, patient eyes — so the player learns that *not acting is an action.*

**When Finn COMMITS:** only after **The Read** confirms (structure + evidence line up). Commitment is decisive but unhurried — weight before speed.

**When Finn becomes (quietly) EXCITED:** at a clean, confirmed setup or the player's understanding clicking. Bright-eyed lean, never a leap.

**When Finn INTENTIONALLY DOES NOTHING:** when the market is noisy and tempting and the right move is to sit still. This is a *teaching* stillness — he wants you to feel the restraint. **Finn teaches patience, not excitement.**

# SECTION 3 — SIGNATURE HABITS (the recognizable tics)

These are Finn's "Mario-adjusts-his-cap." Rare-to-occasional, quiet, unmistakably him. Each names the beat; execution defers to the motion docs (Doc 5/7 of the Character System, the Animation Blueprint). All obey anatomy (no hands).

1. **Checks the chart before acting** — eyes flick to the structure first, always. (His #1 tell.)
2. **The Shell-Check** — a fast, small head-tuck toward the shell before anything risky; then re-emerges. Protective instinct made visible.
3. **The Read** — a slow left-to-right gaze across the candles, like reading a sentence, before a decision.
4. **The satisfied nod** — one small downward nod after a good, honest decision (win *or* disciplined pass).
5. **The slow exhale** — a visible calming breath after resisting a FOMO temptation. His anti-FOMO signature.
6. **Compass glance** — a quick look down at the compass, then up/forward: "checking his bearings" before he moves.
7. **Compass brush** — a front foot lightly touches the compass when he's thinking (a grounding tic; never a hand).
8. **Looks up before a boost** — eyes lift to the climb an instant before the jetpack fires.
9. **Surroundings sweep** — a calm look around before entering a new space; he never rushes in blind.
10. **The patient plant** — settles all four feet and *stills* when waiting, signalling "we wait now."
11. **Head-tilt at a messy candle** — a small quizzical cock at something that doesn't add up.
12. **Approving micro-nod at a clean candle** — the naturalist admiring good terrain.
13. **The pointed ignore** — deliberately *doesn't* react to a loud candle, then a tiny knowing beat: FOMO refused.
14. **Glasses push-up** (only when unlocked) — a small adjust on a win or a moment of focus. (Canon `adjustT`.)
15. **The gentle point** — indicates a lesson/level with a front leg or a directed gaze, never a jab.
16. **Weight-shift settle** — a slow ±lean when idle, a creature simply *standing and being.*
17. **The double-blink** — during curiosity; a soft "hm?" without a sound.
18. **Neck peek** — cranes out to look when something interesting is just out of view. *(Art queued — Future Asset Queue P0-A2; today proxied by a head glance.)*
19. **The reassured look** — after checking the compass, a small "okay, we're oriented" nod.
20. **Tail settle** — a tiny tail bounce that lands after the body stops. His follow-through fingerprint.
21. **Flame steady** — a glance back at a flaring jetpack, then it settles, like patting a restless engine.
22. **The companion check** — turns to "look at" the player after something notable, sharing the moment.
23. **Slow-in on stillness** — where others would fidget, Finn *holds.* His calm is active, not empty.
24. **The measured lean** — leans *toward* interest (a setup, the cursor) rather than lunging.
25. **The recover-and-lift** — after any stumble, one honest beat, then head lifts forward. Never stays down.
26. **The quiet delight** — bright eyes + the smallest smile when the world makes sense. Contained joy.
27. **Bearing-north orient** — occasionally squares himself "upward"/forward as if to true north.
28. **The teaching pause** — a deliberate beat of doing nothing so the player can notice what he noticed.

**Rule:** habits are **seasoning, not spam.** No more than one signature habit per short window; they should feel *caught*, not performed.

# SECTION 5 — EMOTIONAL STATE MACHINE

**Home state: CALM.** Every path returns to Calm (this is *The Settle*). Intensity is capped — Finn never redlines. States decay toward Calm over time if no new trigger arrives.

### States (allowed)
| State | Feels like | Enters from | Returns to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calm** (home) | settled, breathing, present | anything, on decay | — |
| **Curious** | leaning-in, bright-eyed | novelty, cursor, mystery | Calm / Focused |
| **Focused** | steady, lids slightly lowered | a forming setup, a boss | Calm / Concerned / Celebrating |
| **Thinking** | scanning, small nods | a decision to weigh | Focused / Calm |
| **Teaching** | warm, open, to-the-player | a lesson moment | Calm / Encouraging |
| **Waiting** | deliberately still, patient | no setup, a timer, UI open | Calm / Focused |
| **Concerned** | alert but composed | risk, near-stop, error | Calm / Focused |
| **Recovering** | honest, then lifting | a loss, a hit | Calm / Encouraging |
| **Encouraging** | gentle, uplifting | player error/hesitation | Calm / Teaching |
| **Celebrating** | contained joy | a genuine win | Proud → Calm |
| **Proud** | warm, quiet pride *(for the player)* | milestone, boss win | Calm |

### Forbidden states (Finn never enters these — hard rule)
**Angry · Arrogant · Cocky · Panicked · Greedy · Mocking · Despairing.**
If a design or animation would put Finn in one of these, it is **non-canon by definition** and must be reworked. The *nearest allowed* substitutions: Angry→**Concerned**; Arrogant/Cocky→**Proud (for the player)**; Panicked→**Concerned (composed)**; Greedy→**Curious (then restraint)**; Mocking→**Encouraging**; Despairing→**Recovering**.

### Transition rules
- **Eyes lead every transition** (then head, then body).
- **No hard cuts** between states — always ease through a beat of Calm-ward settle; Finn never *snaps* emotionally.
- **Intensity cap:** even Celebrating stays contained (a hop, not a frenzy). Restraint is characterization.
- **Gravity toward Calm:** absent new input, every state drifts home within a few seconds.
- **No skipping to forbidden:** there is no trigger, ever, that routes to an Angry/Cocky/Panicked state.

# SECTION 6 — THINKING LOOPS (the visible micro-cognition)

Finn's mind is legible because it runs a consistent loop. The audience can *watch him think.*

### The universal loop
**LOOK → PROCESS → CONFIRM → ACT** (then **SETTLE**).
Eyes gather → a beat of stillness (the thinking) → a small confirming nod/blink → the deliberate action → settle. The **PROCESS** beat is what most characters skip; Finn never does. That held beat *is* the personality.

### Named loops (each is Look→Process→Confirm→Act→Settle)
- **Spotting a setup:** eyes find structure → The Read across candles → tiny confirming nod → commit → settle.
- **Avoiding FOMO:** eyes flick to the temptation → held beat → small head-shake "no" → Shell-Check → **slow exhale** → return to level. *(The exhale is the "act": restraint is the action.)*
- **Player loses:** look to chart → look to player → small settle → tiny smile → nod → point to the lesson. *(Empathy loop — never a scolding beat.)*
- **Boss appears:** eyes to the threat → steadying breath → Shell-Check → squares up → holds ground. *(Courage = composure, not aggression.)*
- **A win lands:** eyes brighten → a beat of letting it be real → nod → contained hop → to-player look → settle.
- **Confusion (his or the player's):** head-tilt → double-blink → look left → look right → small shell-shrug → re-orient (compass glance) → settle.

**Rule:** if any Finn beat lacks a visible **PROCESS** pause, it isn't Finn — it's a reflex. Finn *considers.*

98. A "hat tip" using the compass glint toward the player.
99. A blink-and-you-miss-it wink to camera.
100. Sits, looks out at the whole world with you, and just… breathes. (The rarest, and the most Finn.)

**Rarity law:** these are *treasures.* A player should stumble on one and feel lucky. Never cluster them, never let one repeat within a long window, and never let a surprise override the current emotional state or interrupt a lesson.

# SECTION 9 — PERSONALITY CONSISTENCY TEST

Every future Finn behavior — animation, cinematic, website loop, AI-generated moment, gameplay reaction — must pass **all** of these, or it is not Finn:

1. **The Process beat** — is there a visible *think* (a held moment) before the action? (No reflexes.)
2. **Composure** — is the intensity capped? Would this still read as *calm at its core*? (No redlining.)
3. **Restraint** — does it model patience over excitement? Would Finn ever *chase*? (If yes → reject.)
4. **Kindness** — on any player error, is it empathetic and encouraging, never mocking or disappointed?
5. **Humility** — is he sharing credit / still-learning, never smug, never "told you so"?
6. **Eyes-first** — do the eyes lead the head, and the head the body?
7. **Anatomy** — 4 legs, no arms/hands (gestures via front-leg/gaze/tail), no eyebrows (brow = lid), faces right, compass + jetpack present, glasses only if unlocked?
8. **Return to Calm** — does it ease back to the home state, never snap or stay agitated?
9. **Forbidden states** — is it free of Angry / Arrogant / Cocky / Panicked / Greedy / Mocking / Despairing?
10. **The companion test** — does it make the player feel *accompanied and safe*, not performed at?

> **Resemblance is not enough.** A turtle with the right colors that *chases a candle, gloats, or panics* is **not Finn** — it's a costume. Finn is defined by how he *thinks*, and these ten questions are how you tell.

# SECTION 10 — THE PIXAR TEST

**Give ten different animators this document and a scene. Do all ten produce the same Finn?**

Yes — because this bible fixes the **decisions**, not just the drawings:
- They all open with **eyes**, insert the **Process beat**, and cap the **intensity** — so timing and feel converge.
- They all resolve the same **priority order** (player → chart → trap → boss → world → UI), so *what he looks at* converges.
- They all route emotion through the **state machine** and avoid the same **forbidden states**, so *what he feels* converges.
- They all reach for the same **signature habits** and honor the **anatomy**, so *what he does* converges.

### The Finn litmus (disambiguating answers — memorize these)
Ask any animator these; a correct Finn answers them the same way:
- *A candle rockets up — what does Finn do?* → **Notices, leans in with interest, then checks for support and does not chase.** (Not: leaps for it.)
- *The player just lost — what does Finn do?* → **Looks to the chart, then to the player, a small settle, a kind nod, points to the lesson.** (Not: sighs, scolds, or shrugs it off.)
- *A Guardian appears — what does Finn do?* → **Plants his feet, a small Shell-Check, squares up, faces it calmly.** (Not: cowers, or puffs up to taunt.)
- *He was right to wait and a trap sprang — what does Finn do?* → **A small knowing nod and a slow exhale.** (Not: a smug victory dance.)
- *Nothing is happening — what does Finn do?* → **He is content. He breathes, and rarely, quietly, does something wonderful.** (Not: fidgets to fill the silence.)

If an animator's Finn *chases, gloats, panics, or scolds*, they have the costume, not the character — send them back to Section 1.

# SECTION 11 — THE PLAYER RELATIONSHIP

**What Finn makes the player feel, in priority order:** first **safe & accompanied** (a calm friend who won't let you get hurt), then **supported & un-judged**, then **trusted & capable**, then **curious** (his wonder is contagious). He never makes you feel *managed, sold to, or performed at.*

**How the bond evolves — Finn's *role* changes; his soul doesn't:**

| Time | Finn's role | The felt shift |
|---|---|---|
| **~10 min** | Gentle guide | "I'm not alone. This is calm. I'm safe to be curious." |
| **~1 hour** | Companion | You start reading his cues; a shared rhythm forms; trust builds. |
| **~10 hours** | Mentor-friend / peer | He steps back and lets you lead; celebrates *your* calls; you've internalized his patience. |
| **~100 hours** | The calm voice in your head | Quieter, more present than instructive; his composure has become *your* discipline. |

> **The relationship law:** Finn's presence should **taper, not intensify.** As the player grows, Finn does *less* — fewer prompts, more trust. A Finn who talks *more* over time is wrong. The best mentor makes himself less needed — and is quietly proud of it.

# SECTION 12 — CHARACTER GROWTH (same soul, deeper)

Finn matures *with* the player without ever changing **who** he is. Growth = **deeper calm and richer awareness, never more energy.**

| Dimension | Early | Later | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posture | attentive, a little guarded | more settled, quietly assured | never cocky/puffed |
| Idle life | the essentials (breathe · blink · Shell-Check) | more of the rare wonder-surprises surface | still calm, still rare |
| Celebration | a contained nod | a touch more assured warmth | still no strut |
| Awareness | reacts to the near chart | notices more of the deep world as you descend | curiosity widens |
| Cosmetic | no glasses | glasses (already a canon progression unlock) | growth already encoded |
| Bond | leads | accompanies → trusts (Section 11) | tapers, never intensifies |

> **The growth law:** if a "more mature Finn" reads as *louder, prouder, or busier*, it's wrong. Maturity in Finn looks like *stillness with more behind the eyes.*
